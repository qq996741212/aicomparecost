Who I Am

I'm Alex, and I run this site. I'm not a journalist and I'm not affiliated with any AI company — I'm someone who uses these tools every day to get actual work done.

That distinction matters. Most "best AI tools" articles are written by people who spent twenty minutes with a free tier and then ranked fifteen products. I don't do that here.

How I Test

Every tool reviewed on this site goes through the same process:

  1. Real task, not a demo prompt. If I'm testing a writing tool, I use it on

a piece I actually need to ship. If it's an image tool, it's for a real project.

  1. At least two weeks of use. The first-hour impression is almost always wrong.

Tools that look magical in a demo fall apart on edge cases; tools that look boring often turn out to be the ones you keep using.

  1. I note the failures. Every review includes what went wrong — errors,

rate limits, weird output, pricing surprises. A review without a drawback section is marketing, not a review.

  1. Screenshots come from my own screen. No stock images, no marketing

material from the vendor.

What I Cover

  • AI chat and reasoning models — which one for which job, and where the

free tiers actually end

  • Image generation — practical workflows, not prompt-collection dumps
  • Video generation — the most expensive category, so cost control matters most
  • Voice and audio — dubbing, narration, music
  • API access — for developers who want to wire these into their own apps

Independence

I buy most subscriptions myself. When a vendor provides free access, I say so in the review.

Some pages contain affiliate links and advertising. This never changes a verdict — if a tool is bad, I say it's bad, and I've published negative reviews of tools that pay well elsewhere. That's the only way this site is worth anything.
