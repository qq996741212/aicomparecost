What to Send

Found an error in an article? Send the URL and what's wrong. I fix corrections within a day and will credit you if you want.

Want me to review a specific tool? Tell me what it is and what you need it for. If enough people ask about the same tool, it goes to the top of the queue.

Vendor or PR? I do accept review access, but with conditions: I publish negative findings, and I don't do pre-approval on reviews. If that doesn't work for you, we shouldn't work together.

Advertising / sponsorship Direct sponsorships are considered case by case. Note that sponsorship never buys a positive review — sponsored placements are clearly labeled.

Response Time

  • General questions: 2-3 business days
  • Review requests: added to the queue, no guarantee of a reply
