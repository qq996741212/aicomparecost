Last updated: September 18, 2026

This policy explains what data this website collects, why, and what choices you have. It is written to be readable, not to hide things in legal language.

Information We Collect

Information you give us If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply. We do not sell it and we do not add you to any list.

Information collected automatically Like most websites, we collect basic technical data:

  • Pages you visit and how long you stay

  • Browser type, device type, and approximate country

We use this only to understand which articles are useful. We do not build profiles of individual visitors.
